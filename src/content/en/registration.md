# Registration

Early registration before 15.2.2025 will bring a significant discount on the registration fee. The registration will start in november 2025.

[Registration link](https://form.eventos.fi/6ef398314470a4ea)

## Prices:

### Early registration

|  | Doctors | Residents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full course | 580€ | 300€ |
| Half course* | 440€ | 220€ |

### Late registration (after 15.2.2025)

|  | Doctors | Residents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full course | 870€ | 450€ |
| Half course* | 660€ | 330€ |

*the half course is from Sun to Wed or Wed to Fri respectively

### Gala Dinner

The **Gala Dinner** is not included and will cost **100€**

### Non-physicians

If you are not a physician, the course is priced at **960€**

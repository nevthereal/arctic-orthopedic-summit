# 26th Arctic Orthopedic Summit 2025

The [Finnish Orthopedic Association](https://www.soy.fi/koulutus/) invites you to the 26th Arctic Orthopedic Summit, a biannual event dedicated to advancing orthopedic knowledge and practice. Held in Finnish Lapland, the summit gathers experts, researchers, and practitioners worldwide to share insights, innovations, and experiences in orthopedics. You can [register here](/registration).

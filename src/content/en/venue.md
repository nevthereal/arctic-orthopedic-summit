# Venue

### Location: Levi, Lapland

### Congress Venue: Hotel Hullu Poro, Levi Mountain Centre

Participants of the congress will have a variety of accommodation options to choose from, catering to different needs and budgets. All accommodations are conveniently located near the Levi Mountain Centre. The congress itself will be held at the Levi Mountain Centre, within the Hotel Hullu Poro, offering easy access to all congress events. A special, *limited* room quota has been reserved for participants, including hotel rooms and cabins, ensuring flexibility for attendees to find the most suitable option for their stay.

Participants can choose from a range of accommodations conveniently located near the congress venue:

- Hotel Hullu Poro: [hulluporo.fi](https://hulluporo.fi) - with the promo code: <br> **ArcticOrtho2025**
- Cabins and other options: [levi.fi](https://levi.fi)

Both options provide comfortable and accessible lodging for your stay in Levi during the congress.
